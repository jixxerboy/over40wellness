Joint pain after 40 has specific, mostly fixable causes — it isn't simply "wear and tear." Cartilage naturally thins and synovial fluid production slows, but inactivity accelerates stiffness far more than movement does. Sitting too long starves joints of the lubrication they need, cold-tissue warm-ups get skipped, and dehydration dries out cartilage further. None of this is fixed by resting more.
