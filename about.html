About Over 40 Wellness

Honest health advice for real life after 40

Somewhere after 40, things start to change. Sleep gets lighter, energy dips in the afternoon, joints complain more, and remembering where you put your keys becomes a daily puzzle. None of that means you're "past it". It just means your body needs a slightly different approach than it did at 25.

Over 40 Wellness is here to help with that, in plain English and without the hype.

What You'll Find Here

Everything on the site is organised around the four areas that make the biggest difference to how we feel after 40:

Sleep & RecoveryFalling asleep, staying asleep, and waking up refreshed Energy & MetabolismSteady energy, strength and a healthy weight Joint & Pain ReliefStiffness, aches and staying mobile Brain & ClarityFocus, memory and a calmer mind

You'll find practical articles you can act on straight away, and honest product reviews for when you're wondering whether something is worth your money.

How We Review Products

  • We do the research. We look at what's actually in a product, what the evidence says, who makes it, the real price, and the small print on the guarantee.
  • We're clear about what we know and what we don't. If we haven't tried something ourselves, we say so. If the evidence is thin, we say that too.
  • We leave out the hype. No miracle cures, no "doctors hate this", no made-up testimonials. We'd rather you trust us than click on something.
  • We tell you who it's not for. Every review includes who should skip it and when to speak to your GP first.

How This Site Is Funded

Over 40 Wellness is free to read. Some links on the site are affiliate links: if you buy something through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's how we keep the lights on.

Affiliate links are always labelled #ad · affiliate link, and earning a commission never changes what we say about a product. If something isn't right for you, we'll tell you.

What We're Not

We're not doctors, and nothing on this site is medical advice. Our articles are general information to help you have better conversations with your GP or pharmacist, not to replace them. Always check with a professional before starting a new supplement or exercise plan, especially if you take medication or have a health condition.
